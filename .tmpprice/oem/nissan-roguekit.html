2026.5 Nissan Rogue

Where smart tech, versatile space and turbocharged efficiency meet bold family style

July 29, 2025

View More Rogue News

The 2026.5 Rogue will be offered in S (FWD/AWD), SV (FWD/AWD), Rock Creek (AWD), Dark Armor (FWD/AWD) and Platinum (AWD) grades.

WHAT'S NEW                                           

  • Stylish new Dark Armor grade refines Rogue's appearance with black 19-inch wheels and gloss black exterior trim on the grille, mirrors, pillars and roof rails.
  • Joining the lineup above the SV grade, Dark Armor adds HD Intelligent Around View® Monitor with Moving Object Detection4, PrimaTexTM leatherette-appointed and heated front seats, heated steering wheel and a panoramic moonroof.

NEED TO KNOW

DESIGN

  • Platinum grade offers gloss black exterior trim, available chestnut brown semi-aniline leather-appointed upholstery, 19-inch wheels and interior LED ambient lighting.
  • Thoughtful design touches include rear doors that open to nearly 90 degrees, a spot to hold milk jugs (preventing them from sliding in the cargo area) and available rear door sunshades.
  • Bold color palette includes four striking two-tone options: Everest White Pearl, Deep Ocean Blue Pearl, Champagne Silver Metallic and Boulder Gray Pearl can each be paired with a Super Black roof.

TECH

  • Available Google built-in5 brings familiar apps, including intuitive navigation with Google Maps, right to the dashboard.
  • Stay connected with standard Apple CarPlay® and Android Auto™, four USB-C ports (SV and higher) and available wireless phone charging.
  • Helpful convenience technologies include standard Rear Door Alert6, Easy-Fill Tire Alert and available remote engine start7.

SAFETY AND DRIVER ASSISTANCE

  • Advanced camera technology, including available HD Intelligent Around View® Monitor5 with Off-Road View and Moving Object Detection5, assist with parking or avoiding obstacles.
  • Simplified road trips with available ProPILOT Assist8, providing steering assistance to help make highway driving more convenient.
  • The reassurance of available Nissan Safety Shield® 3604 includes Automatic Emergency Braking with Pedestrian Detection, Blind Spot Warning, Rear Cross Traffic Alert, Lane Departure Warning and more.

PERFORMANCE

  • Rock Creek enables adventure beyond the pavement with Falken Wild Peak all-terrain tires, Hill Descent Control, tubular roof rack and unique front fascia.
  • Advanced VC-Turbo engine delivers best-in-class standard gas engine torque9 with fuel economy of up to 29 mpg city, 36 mpg highway and 32 mpg combined (S/SV FWD).
  • Conquer varied conditions more confidently with available Intelligent All-Wheel Drive4 featuring five drive modes (Standard, Eco, Sport, Off-Road, Snow).
