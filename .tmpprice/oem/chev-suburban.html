    CAPABILITY

    Power up

    Suburban offers exceptional performance and handling thanks to available features like Magnetic Ride Control, Four-Corner Air Ride Adaptive Suspension and three distinct powertrains, each bringing a balance of power, efficiency and capability.

    Duramax® 3.0L Turbo Diesel

    Available on LT, RST, Z71, Premier and High Country, the Duramax 3.0L Turbo-Diesel engine offers up to 728 miles of EPA-estimated maximum highway range per tank,* 305 horsepower and 495 lb.-ft. of torque, so it’s always ready to go

    5.3L EcoTec3 V8

    Standard on all models except High Country, the 5.3L EcoTec3 V8 engine with Dynamic Fuel Management delivers 355 horsepower and 383 lb.-ft. of torque to give you the power to do what you love.

    6.2L EcoTec3 V8

    Standard on High Country and available on RST, Z71 and Premier, the 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 with Dynamic Fuel Management delivers 420 horsepower and 460 lb.-ft. of torque to haul everything from your family to your trailer.

    SUBURBAN FAQs

    How much does a 2026 Chevy Suburban weigh?

    The weight of a 2026 Suburban can vary depending on the model. Here’s the weight range:
    5,680–6,053 lbs.

    How long is a 2026 Chevy Suburban?

    The length of a 2026 Chevy Suburban is 226.3 inches.

    How tall is a 2026 Chevy Suburban?

    The height of a 2026 Chevy Suburban ranges between:
    75.6–76.6 inches

    How wide is a 2026 Chevy Suburban?

    The 2026 Chevy Suburban is 93.6 inches, including the mirrors.

    How much does a 2026 Chevy Suburban cost?

    The MSRP of a 2026 Chevy Suburban depends on the model and any additional available features selected, but the base MSRP for the Chevy Suburban LS 2WD is $63,700.*

    Where is the 2026 Chevy Suburban assembled?

    The 2026 Chevy Suburban is assembled at the Arlington Assembly plant in Arlington, Texas.*

    How many seats does a 2026 Chevy Suburban have?

    There are 8 standard* seats in the 2026 Chevy Suburban, but other available configurations include:

    • 7 available seats
    • 9 available seats

    How much can a 2026 Chevy Suburban tow?

    The maximum available towing capacity of a 2026 Chevy Suburban is 8,200 lbs.*

    What kind of gas does a 2026 Chevy Suburban take?

    The type of gas the 2026 Chevy Suburban takes depends on the engine. Here are the recommended fuel types for each engine:

    • Regular 87 Octane for the 5.3L EcoTec®3 V8
    • Premium 91 Octane for the 6.2L EcoTec3 V8
    • TOP TIER® Diesel for the Duramax® 3.0L Turbo-Diesel

    Is the 2026 Chevy Suburban an SUV?

    Yes. Chevy offers a variety of SUVs, including Trax, Equinox, Traverse, etc. Among them, the 2026 Chevy Suburban is considered a full-size SUV.

    How much cargo space does a 2026 Chevy Suburban have?

    Cargo space in the 2026 Chevy Suburban is configurable and depends on placement within the vehicle. Measurements include:

    • 144.5 cu.-ft. behind the 1st row*
    • 93.8 cu.-ft. behind the 2nd row*
    • 41.5 cu.-ft behind the 3rd row*

    How much horsepower does a 2026 Chevy Suburban have?

    Maximum horsepower in the 2026 Chevy Suburban depends on the engine. Here are the specifics for each:

    • 420 HP: available 6.2L EcoTec3 V8
    • 355 HP: standard 5.3L EcoTec3 V8
    • 305 HP: Duramax® 3.0L Turbo-Diesel
