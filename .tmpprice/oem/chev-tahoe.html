    PERFORMANCE

    Reach your full potential

    Your possibilities are endless with three engine options to choose from, each giving you the performance to go anywhere. Revel in exceptional ride and handling with available features like Magnetic Ride Control and Four-Corner Air Ride Adaptive Suspension that can help optimize ride height and comfort by adjusting the suspension to road conditions. 

    Duramax® 3.0L Turbo-Diesel

    Available on LT, RST, Z71, Premier and High Country, the Duramax 3.0L Turbo-Diesel engine offers 624 miles of EPA-estimated maximum highway range per tank,* 305 horsepower and 495 lb.-ft. of torque, so it’s always ready to go. 

    5.3L EcoTec3 V8

    Standard on all models except High Country, the 5.3L EcoTec3 V8 engine with Dynamic Fuel Management delivers 355 horsepower and 383 lb.-ft. of torque to give you the power to do what you love.

    6.2L EcoTec3 V8

    Standard on High Country and available on RST, Z71 and Premier, the 6.2L EcoTec3 V8 with Dynamic Fuel Management delivers 420 horsepower and 460 lb.-ft. of torque to haul everything from your family to your trailer.

    TAHOE FAQs

    How much does a 2026 Chevy Tahoe weigh?

    The weight of a 2026 Tahoe can vary depending on the model. Here’s the weight range:

    • 5,509–5,882 lbs.

    How long is a 2026 Chevy Tahoe?

    The length of a 2026 Chevy Tahoe is 211.3 inches.

    How tall is a 2026 Chevy Tahoe?

    The height of a 2026 Chevy Tahoe ranges between:

    • 75.8–76.8 inches

    How wide is a 2026 Chevy Tahoe?

    The 2026 Chevy Tahoe is 93.6 inches, including the mirrors.

    How much does a 2026 Chevy Tahoe cost?

    The MSRP of a 2026 Chevy Tahoe depends on the model and any additional available features selected, but the base MSRP for the Chevy Tahoe LS 2WD is $60,700.*

    Where is the 2026 Chevy Tahoe assembled?

    The 2026 Chevy Tahoe is assembled at the Arlington Assembly plant in Arlington, Texas.*

    How many seats does a 2026 Chevy Tahoe have?

    There are 8 standard* seats in the 2026 Chevy Tahoe, but other available configurations include:

    • 7 available seats
    • 9 available seats

    How much can a 2026 Chevy Tahoe tow?

    The maximum available towing capacity of a 2026 Chevy Tahoe is 8,400 lbs.*

    What kind of gas does a 2026 Chevy Tahoe take?

    The type of gas the 2026 Chevy Tahoe takes depends on the engine. Here are the recommended fuel types for each engine:

    • Regular 87 Octane for the 5.3L EcoTec®3 V8
    • Premium 91 Octane for the 6.2L EcoTec3 V8
    • TOP TIER® Diesel for the Duramax® 3.0L Turbo-Diesel

    Do the seats fold down in a 2026 Chevy Tahoe?

    Yes. The second— and third—row seats fold flat in the 2026 Chevy Tahoe.

    Does the Chevy Tahoe have captain's chairs?

    In a 2026 Chevy Tahoe, captain’s chairs are available on the LT, RST and Z71 models. They are standard on the Premier and High Country.

    What are the different models of the 2026 Chevy Tahoe?

    The 2026 Chevy Tahoe is available in the following models:

    • LS
    • LT
    • RST
    • Z71
    • Premier
    • High Country
