    Safety and driver assistance*

    Your safety is our greatest priority, and Enclave is designed and engineered with that in mind.

    Front Pedestrian and Bicyclist Braking*

    Can help you avoid or reduce the severity of a collision with a pedestrian or bicyclist it detects directly in front of you. It provides alerts and can automatically provide hard emergency braking or enhance the driver’s hard braking. It has limited nighttime and low visibility performance.

    Blind Zone Steering Assist*

    Can help alert you and provide a brief, firm turn of the steering wheel when a potential crash is detected with a moving vehicle in the lane you’re entering.

    Intersection Automatic Emergency Braking*

    Can help you avoid or reduce the severity of an intersection collision with a detected cross-traffic vehicle. It provides alerts and can even automatically provide hard emergency braking or enhance the driver’s hard braking.

    Rear Cross Traffic Braking*

    Alerts you and automatically provides hard emergency braking to help prevent a collision with detected vehicles crossing behind you.

    Side Bicyclist Alert*

    Helps alert you to a detected bicyclist that is approaching a side blind zone. Also, during a brief time after parking, the feature can alert you when a detected bicyclist is approaching so you don’t open your door.
