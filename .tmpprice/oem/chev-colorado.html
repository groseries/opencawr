    DRIVE MODES

    In command, on demand

    Colorado offers up to five available drive modes to help fine-tune your truck’s performance and adjust to challenging road conditions with a turn of the dial.

    Off-Road*

    Offers more traction on loose surfaces like grass, mud, gravel or deep snow.

    Terrain*

    Perfect for tackling steep hills or crawling over obstacles. Can also automatically apply the brakes for enhanced climbing control.

    Baja

    Exclusive to ZR2, this mode optimizes power delivery, stability and traction control when driving over sandy terrain.

    Available Tow/Haul

    If equipped, optimizes performance when towing or hauling a heavy load.

    Normal

    The default mode that optimizes performance for your everyday drive.

    COLORADO FAQs

    What is the maximum Chevy Colorado towing capacity?

    Every 2026 Chevy Colorado is built to tow heavy loads, with a maximum available trailering weight of 7,700 lbs.*

    How much does Chevy Colorado weigh? 

    The weight of a 2026 Chevy Colorado can vary depending on factors such as optional equipment and whether it's loaded with cargo. Here's the weight range for an unloaded Chevy Colorado: 

     

    • 4,340 lbs.–5,260 lbs. 

    How long is Chevy Colorado?

    2026 Colorado measures 214 inches in length. 

    How wide is Chevy Colorado?

    The width of a 2026 Chevy Colorado is 84.4 inches including the mirrors.  

    How tall is Chevy Colorado?

    The height of a 2026 Chevy Colorado can vary based on several factors, but here are the basics:

     

    • 78.8 inches–82.7 inches 

    How much does Chevy Colorado cost?

    The MSRP of a 2026 Chevy Colorado depends on the type of truck and any additional available features selected, but the starting at MSRP is $32,400,* depending on trim. 

    What year did Chevy Colorado come out?

    Chevy Colorado was originally introduced in 2004. After a brief stop in production, Colorado made its return in 2015.  

    What engine does the Chevy Colorado have?

    Every 2026 Colorado is powered by the durable, capable 2.7L TurboMax® I-4 engine.  

    Does Chevy Colorado come with a V6?

    With the 2.7L TurboMax I-4 providing 310 horsepower and 430 lb.-ft. of torque, 2026 Colorado does not offer a V6 at this time.  

    How much horsepower does Chevy Colorado have?

     The standard 2.7L TurboMax I-4 engine generates 310 horsepower. 

    What kind of gas does Chevy Colorado take?

    The standard 2.7L TurboMax I-4 engine requires regular unleaded gasoline with a minimum of 87 octane. 

    Is Chevy Colorado a full-size truck?

    Chevy Colorado is our fully capable midsize pickup. If you’re looking for a full-size pickup, check out Chevy Silverado.  

    What does Z71 mean on Chevy Colorado?

     Z71 has long stood for legendary off-road capability. To live up to that standard, Colorado Z71 includes:  

     

    • 2.7L TurboMax engine with 310 horsepower and 430 lb.-ft. of torque 
    • 18-inch wheels with 32-inch all-terrain tires
    • Jet Black interior with Adrenaline Red accents 
    • Four selectable drive modes and off-road performance display 
    • LED headlamps, taillamps and fog lamps
