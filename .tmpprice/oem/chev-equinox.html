    EQUINOX FAQs

    How long is the Chevy Equinox?

    The overall length of the 2027 Chevy Equinox is 183.2" (just over 15 feet). 

    How much does a Chevy Equinox weigh?

    The weight of the 2027 Chevy Equinox can vary depending on factors such as drive type, trim level, optional equipment and whether it’s loaded with cargo. The weight range for an unloaded 2027 Chevy Equinox is between 3,428 lbs. and 3,629 lbs. 

    How many seats are in a Chevy Equinox?

    The 2027 Chevy Equinox seats 5.

    Is the Chevy Equinox AWD?

    AWD is available on the 2027 Chevy Equinox.

    How much can the Chevy Equinox tow?

    When properly equipped, the 2027 Chevy Equinox can tow up to 1,500 lbs.*
